FAQ

Common questions.

What is MindX AI and how does it help my store?+
MindX AI is an AI agent that connects to your Shopify store and handles customer support and chat end to end — automatically. It reads live order data, understands what each customer needs, and sends complete resolutions without drafts or a human approval queue. You set the limits once; MindX AI works within them, 24/7.
What counts as one conversation?+
One conversation is one bounded interaction with a single customer, in a single channel, about a single issue — counted once from the first message to closure. A chat that escalates to a ticket is still one conversation, never billed twice.
Does MindX AI send responses, or queue drafts for me to approve?+
MindX AI sends complete resolutions autonomously — no approval queue, no draft waiting for review. If you want to limit what the agent does autonomously, set your rules in the Protect step: refund ceilings, discount caps, cancellation rules. Anything outside your limits comes to you.
What if the AI gets something wrong?+
Every action that touches money is checked against your rules before it happens. The system fails closed — if the check can't complete, the action is blocked and escalated to you, never silently allowed. If overall resolution quality falls below the published floor, you receive automatic credits.
How long does setup actually take?+
Under 5 minutes. Install from the Shopify App Store, authorize the connection in one screen, and MindX AI trains itself automatically on your store's products, orders, and policies. No prompts to write, no knowledge base to build.
How is this different from Shopify Inbox or a basic chatbot?+
Shopify Inbox and basic chatbots answer scripted FAQs. They cannot look up a real order, check live tracking, or process a return tied to your actual store data. MindX AI is connected to your live Shopify store and takes real actions within the rules you set.
What channels are supported right now?+
Currently: Support (Shopify inbox) and Chat (live chat on your store). WhatsApp Business is launching next week. More channels are on the roadmap.
Why are there two separate guarantees?+
Because they protect different things. The Resolution Rate Guarantee protects AI quality — automatic credits if we fall short. The 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee protects your payment — full refund within 30 days, no questions. Merging them into one vague promise makes both weaker. We keep them separate on purpose.
