FAQ

Common questions.

What is WISMO?+
WISMO means "Where Is My Order?" It covers customer questions about order status, shipment progress, tracking information, and expected delivery. It is one of the highest-volume, most repetitive workflows in ecommerce customer support.
Does MindX work with Shopify?+
Yes. Shopify is the first commerce platform supported by MindX. Connect your store from the Shopify App Store in under five minutes — no code needed.
Does MindX replace my support team?+
No. MindX is designed to handle repetitive, well-defined workflows automatically and escalate cases that require human judgment — with full context attached so your team can pick up immediately.
What is an AI Resolution?+
An AI Resolution is a customer conversation that MindX successfully handles according to the defined workflow and outcome criteria. One Resolution is one bounded interaction with a single customer, in a single channel, about a single issue — counted once from first message to closure. Never billed twice.
What happens when MindX doesn't know the answer?+
MindX doesn't guess. It can ask the customer for clarification, or escalate the conversation to a human agent with the full context already attached — so your team doesn't start from scratch.
Does MindX access my order data?+
MindX uses the connected Shopify data required to resolve the WISMO workflow — customer identity, order details, fulfilment status, and live carrier tracking. It retrieves only what is needed for each specific request.
Can I use MindX for other support workflows?+
The initial product is focused on WISMO. Returns, refunds, and other customer service workflows are on the roadmap and will be introduced as MindX expands from this proven foundation.
Will MindX support other ecommerce platforms?+
Shopify is the starting platform. Additional commerce platforms are part of the longer-term roadmap. We are focused on proving the outcome on Shopify first.
